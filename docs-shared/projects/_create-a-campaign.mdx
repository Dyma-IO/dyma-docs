## 4. Create a campaign

The wizard has six steps: **Basics → Quests → Rewards & distribution → Eligibility → Fund escrow
(automatic mode only) → Review & launch**. Read [Campaign lifecycle](./campaign-lifecycle) for what
happens next and [Distribution modes and the split](./distribution) before choosing a mode.

The **Eligibility** step decides who can take part: a verified e-mail, a minimum account age,
linked accounts, a minimum reputation tier (only earners from that tier up, so newcomers cannot
join) and a private mode, where only the accounts you invite can join and the campaign stays out
of Discover. Each quest can also have **unlock conditions** (its *Conditions* section): another
quest completed or not, after or before a date, a cap on participants, or a minimum reputation
tier, all of them or any one. Earners see each rule, and whether they meet it, before they start.
