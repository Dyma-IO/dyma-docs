# Overview

Dyma serves three audiences. Pick the path that matches your role.

## Earners

Use the earner app ([app.dyma.io](https://app.dyma.io)) to discover campaigns, complete quests
and collect points and token rewards.

1. Sign in with a wallet (Keplr, Leap, Cosmostation, EightSaf, or a mobile wallet through
   WalletConnect), or with Google, X, GitHub, Discord or an e-mail code.
2. Open a live campaign and complete its quests. Each quest says how it is checked.
3. Points are credited **as soon as a quest is verified** — instantly for automatic checks, after
   review for quests a moderator approves.
4. When the campaign ends and has a token pool, your share is computed from your points and paid
   to your payout wallet on the campaign's chain.

Read [How earning works](../earners/how-it-works), then [Wallets and payout address](../earners/wallets).

## Project teams

Use [Studio](https://studio.dyma.io) to run campaigns.

1. Sign up, create your **project** and submit it for review (projects are approved
   automatically unless Dyma switched that off).
2. Create a **campaign** with the wizard: basics and dates, quests, reward pool and distribution
   mode, eligibility, escrow funding (automatic mode only), review and launch.
3. Dyma reviews the campaign; once approved it is scheduled and starts at its start date.
4. Review submissions that need a human, watch analytics, export CSVs.
5. After the end, pay rewards (manual mode) or follow the escrow while it pays everyone
   (automatic mode).

Read [Getting started in Studio](../projects/getting-started) and
[Distribution modes and the split](../projects/distribution).
