# For contributors

Contributors earn **DP (Dyma Points)**, build **DR (Dima Reputation)**, and may receive on-chain rewards from campaign escrow.

## Wallet and identity

- Connect a Cosmos-compatible wallet to start earning.
- Optional email linking uses wallet-signed messages; email is never shared with projects.
- Social accounts (X, Discord, GitHub) may be required for specific quest types.

## Reputation and points

| Token | Purpose |
|-------|---------|
| **DP** | Universal reward currency and season scoring |
| **DR** | Soulbound reputation NFT on Safrochain; levels unlock multipliers and access |

Reputation grows from validated completions, consistency, and sybil-resistance signals. Levels can require a gas-paid upgrade on-chain.

## Seasons, streaks, and guilds

- **Seasons** — Time-boxed scoring periods with featured campaigns and rank tiers.
- **Streaks** — Daily completion multipliers (e.g. a 12-day streak may apply a 1.35× bonus).
- **Guilds** — Team coordination with pooled streaks and referral benefits.

## Claiming rewards

On-chain campaign rewards route from audited escrow to your wallet address. Dyma does not hold custody between verification and settlement.

For off-chain or DP-only campaigns, projects may define separate claim rules in campaign details.

## Tips

- Read quest instructions carefully; manual tasks need complete submissions.
- Check verification method and expected SLA before starting time-sensitive quests.
- Report suspicious campaigns via in-app tools or [Discussions](https://github.com/orgs/Dyma-IO/discussions).
