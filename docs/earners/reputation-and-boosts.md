# Reputation and boosts

Points (DP) reward each quest. **Dyma Reputation** rewards staying active over time: you convert
the DP you collect into reputation, your reputation places you in a tier, and some campaigns give
higher tiers a larger share of their pool.

## How reputation rises

| Source | Reputation |
|---|---|
| Converting DP | 1 reputation per DP by default, from 50 DP |
| A friend you invited qualifies | 25 by default ([Invite friends](./invite-friends)) |

Converting moves DP out of your balance: the balance goes back to zero (or down by the amount you
chose) and your reputation goes up. Your points **in each campaign** do not change, so converting
never lowers a reward you are earning. Reputation never decays.

If a review later takes back a submission whose DP you already converted, the matching reputation
is taken back too. It shows in your history.

## Tiers

| Tier | From | Boost |
|---|---|---|
| Newcomer | 0 | x1.00 |
| Explorer | 250 | x1.05 |
| Contributor | 1,000 | x1.10 |
| Builder | 3,000 | x1.20 |
| Trailblazer | 7,500 | x1.35 |
| Champion | 15,000 | x1.50 |
| Legend | 30,000 | x1.75 |
| Mythic | 60,000 | x2.00 |

These are the defaults; Dyma can change tiers, thresholds and boosts. The **Reputation** page shows
your tier, your progress to the next one, your rank among earners and your history, and has a
reputation leaderboard. Tier chips appear next to names on campaign leaderboards.

## Boosts

A campaign with the **reputation boost** on (the campaign page says so under *How rewards are
split*) multiplies each qualifying earner's weight by their tier's boost when the split is
computed. With the same points, a Builder (x1.20) receives 20% more weight than a Newcomer. The
boost never lets you qualify for rules you do not meet (minimum points, top N) and never changes
your points.

The tier that counts is the one you have when the split is computed (the end of the review grace).

## The on-chain badge

Your tier can also live in your wallet as a **soulbound badge** on Safrochain (an NFT that cannot
be transferred or sold):

1. On the Reputation page, choose **Claim badge** (or **Upgrade badge** after a new tier).
2. Connect one of your linked Safrochain wallets and approve the transaction (a small SAF fee).
3. The badge for your current tier is minted to that wallet. An upgrade burns your previous badge
   in the same transaction.

Newcomers have no badge. One wallet holds the badge of one account only. While Dyma has not
opened badge claims yet, the page shows "Coming soon".
