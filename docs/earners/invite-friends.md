# Invite friends

Every earner has one invite link for the whole platform, in **Profile → Invite friends**:
`https://app.dyma.io/login?ref=<your code>`. Copy it or share it from the page.

## How a referral counts

1. Your friend opens the link and creates a **new** Dyma account (wallet, Google, X, GitHub,
   Discord or e-mail). An existing account cannot be referred, nobody can refer themselves, and a
   chain of referrals cannot loop.
2. Your friend completes **3 different verified quests within 30 days** of signing up, with an
   active account.
3. The referral qualifies, once:

| Who | Reward |
|---|---|
| You | **100 DP** and **25 reputation** |
| Your friend | a **50 DP** welcome bonus |

At most **10 referrals** of one inviter are rewarded per day (UTC). Referrals above the limit still
show in your list as *Daily limit reached*, without a reward. These are the defaults: Dyma can
change every value in its admin console.

## Your list

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| In progress | your friend has until the date shown to complete the quests |
| Rewarded | qualified, rewards credited |
| Daily limit reached | qualified above the daily limit: no reward |
| Expired | the 30 days ended first |
| Revoked | Dyma took the reward back (abuse) |

Names are masked unless the friend has a public username. A friend you invited sees their welcome
bonus progress on the same page.

## Campaign referral quests

Some campaigns also have a **referral quest** that counts the sign-ups you bring during that
campaign. It uses the same link and is completed inside the campaign, independently of the
platform program above.

## Rewards and splits

Referral DP count in your DP balance (you can convert them into reputation) and on the global
leaderboard. They never count in a campaign's token split, which only uses the points earned in
that campaign.
