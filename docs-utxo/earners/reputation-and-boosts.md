## The on-chain badge

Your tier can also live in your Cardano wallet as a **reputation badge**: a CIP-68 token that names
your wallet's key and counts only while it stays in that wallet.

1. On the Reputation page, choose **Claim badge** (after a new tier: **Upgrade to** that tier; after
   new artwork: **Refresh badge**).
2. Connect a linked wallet whose payment address you proved, and approve the transaction. Your
   wallet pays the network fee and the ADA kept with the badge record.
3. The badge for your current tier goes to that wallet. An upgrade or a refresh retires your
   previous badge in the same transaction.

Newcomers have no badge. While Dyma UTXO has not opened badge claims, the page shows them as coming
soon. How the badge works: [Reputation badge (CIP-68)](./reputation-badge).
